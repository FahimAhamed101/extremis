  • Policies and standards

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?

Open the login page and choose the forgotten password option. We will email you a secure link to set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Close Account. You can also contact support and we will remove your data in line with our privacy policy.

How do I report a post or a user?

Use the options menu on any post or profile and select Report. Our moderation team reviews every report within 24 hours.

Why can I not upload a photo or video?

Uploads usually fail because of an unsupported file type or a slow connection. Check the format and size limits, then try again.
